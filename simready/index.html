SimReady Produce

Six food produce assets from NVIDIA's SimReady collection. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom. Sizes and masses come from each asset's SimReady metadata.

Assets: nvidia/simready-assets by NVIDIA, licensed CC BY 4.0. Changes: the visual mesh and its textures were converted from USD to glTF (GLB) for the browser, textures capped at 1024 px; physics and the MDL materials are not included.
